MARCH MAD - Court Zones

Proposed shooting/dunking accuracy by zone

CURRENT (Broken)

  • Dunk zone: tiny 1.93u sliver, never triggers
  • Dunk success: 70-90% random (should be 100%)
  • All defenders penalize accuracy just by existing nearby
  • Standing defenders count as "contest"
  • defenderDistance penalty: up to 40% at point blank

PROPOSED (Fun)

  • Dunk zone: entire paint area (5.8u deep)
  • Dunk success: 100% uncontested, always
  • Only active guard (G key) penalizes accuracy
  • Must be close to shooter AND guarding to contest
  • Dunk over standing defender = knockdown

Key changes from current system:
- Defender distance penalty REMOVED — only active guard (G key animation) affects accuracy
- Guard must be within 2 units of shooter AND actively guarding to contest (-20%)
- Jump-block within 3 units AND actively blocking to contest (-30%)
- Dunks are ALWAYS 100% unless jump-blocked at the rim
- Dunking over a standing defender knocks them down (fall animation)
- Base accuracies bumped up to make the game more fun and arcade-y
- Layup accuracy raised from 75% to 85%
- Mid-range raised from 45% to 55%
- Three-pointer raised from 33% to 40%
